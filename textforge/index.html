Why TextForge

The 80% of writing help you need, without the monthly bill.

Most of what we use Grammarly or QuillBot for — cleaning up emails, summarizing, adjusting tone — doesn't need a cloud GPU farm. A small model on your laptop handles it just fine. So why are we still paying for it?

01

Your text, your machine

Every request goes to http://localhost by default. Nothing is sent to any third party. Verify it yourself in the network inspector.

02

No subscriptions, no caps

Rewrite as many emails as you want. There's no token limit, no rate ceiling, no "upgrade to Premium" wall. You already paid for your laptop.

03

Pick your own model

TextForge auto-detects every model installed on your local AI server. Llama, Mistral, Qwen, Phi, Gemma — pick what suits your hardware and writing style.

04

Preview before replace

Nothing touches your text without confirmation. Every rewrite opens in a preview pane where you can edit, retry, copy, or reject.

05

Works where you write

Gmail compose, LinkedIn, X, GitHub, support forms, comment boxes — anywhere there's a textarea or rich-text editor, the icon shows up.

06

Remote-friendly

Running Ollama on a beefier workstation or homelab box? Point the extension at any endpoint over your network — Tailscale-friendly too.

How it works

Up and running in under five minutes.

1

Install Ollama

Download ollama.com — free, open source, works great on Apple Silicon and Linux. Or use LM Studio.

2

Pull a model

Run ollama pull llama3.2:3b in your terminal. Pick a size that suits your RAM.

3

Install TextForge

Add the extension from the Chrome Web Store. It'll auto-detect your installed models.

4

Click and write

The icon appears on any textarea. Click it, pick an action, preview, accept. Done.

Pick your model

From a 3B on your MacBook Air to a 70B on your workstation.

TextForge auto-detects every model installed on your local server and shows them in a dropdown — with parameter size, quantization, and disk size. Match the model to your machine.
